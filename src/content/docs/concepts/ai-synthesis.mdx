One of the most powerful features of VoxTrade is that **no spoken commitment is lost**. 

Whether two human founders are negotiating a partnership in **User ↔ User** mode or a buyer is ordering inventory in **User ↔ Agent** mode, the protocol continuously logs, analyzes, and synthesizes the conversation into structured deliverables.

---

## The AI Synthesis Pipeline

```
[ Raw Audio Stream ] ──> [ Live Speech-to-Text ] ──> [ Timestamped Transcript Log ]
                                                                │
[ On-Chain Soroban Escrow ] <── [ Structured Agreement ] <──────┘
```

<Steps>

1. **Continuous Audio Logging**
   As audio chunks are streamed, the speech recognition engine generates real-time transcripts with millisecond-precision timestamps:
   ```json
   {
     "id": "utt_01",
     "speaker": "buyer",
     "timestamp": "00:01:24.400",
     "text": "Can you review our smart contract for 120 XLM by Thursday?"
   }
   ```

2. **Semantic Agreement Extraction**
   When speakers reach consensus, the synthesis agent evaluates the entire conversation context to extract key commercial variables:
   - **Scope of Work**: Specific deliverables agreed upon.
   - **Counterparties**: Buyer address and Seller address.
   - **Total Consideration**: Escrow amount in XLM or USDC.
   - **Milestones**: Incremental checkpoints (e.g., 50% upon draft, 50% upon final delivery).
   - **Dispute & Timeout Windows**: Stellar ledger expiration height.

3. **Contract Draft Generation**
   The synthesis engine outputs a human-readable legal summary displayed right on the call screen:
   ```markdown
   # COMMERCIAL SERVICE AGREEMENT
   - Date: 2026-09-12
   - Buyer: GAB...7X2
   - Seller: GCY...9M4
   - Deliverable: Rust Soroban Smart Contract Security Review
   - Agreed Compensation: 120.0000000 XLM
   - Expiration Ledger: 104,200 (~24 hours)
   - Settlement Hashlock: 0x7f83b165...
   ```

4. **Transcript Export & Download**
   Both counterparties can immediately click **"Download Transcript (.json)"** to store an encrypted, timestamped archive of the call for their records.

</Steps>

## Verification & Non-Repudiation

How do we prove that a transcript matches the on-chain escrow?

1. The entire JSON transcript is serialized into canonical JSON (RFC 8785).
2. The SHA-256 hash of the canonical transcript is computed:
   ```
   transcriptHash = SHA-256(CanonicalTranscript)
   ```
3. This hash is embedded into the Soroban escrow memo or contract storage upon creation.
4. Anyone can verify the transcript was not tampered with by hashing the downloaded JSON file and comparing it against the on-chain transaction footprint on Stellar Expert.

<Aside type="tip" title="Privacy Preservation">
Transcripts are kept strictly on the client side or in temporary ephemeral session memory unless explicitly saved by the participants. No conversation logs are sold or sent to third-party ad networks.
</Aside>
