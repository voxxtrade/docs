The **`voxtrade-app`** web application (built on Next.js 14, Tailwind CSS, and Lucide icons) serves as the reference implementation and visual testbench for the VoxTrade protocol.

---

## Testbench Architecture

Located at `/app`, the testbench interface is divided into three key panels:

```

│                          VOXTRADE TESTBENCH                            │
├────────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────┬─────────────┤
│ 1. AUDIO & TELEMETRY       │ 2. LIVE TRANSCRIPT          │ 3. CONTRACT │

└────────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────────┴─────────────┘
```

## Real-Time Audio Telemetry Pipeline

The browser connects to your physical microphone using the `Web Audio API`:

1. **Audio Context**: Initializes `AudioContext` with `sampleRate: 44100` or `48000`.
2. **Analyser Node**: An `AnalyserNode` with `fftSize: 64` produces 32 distinct frequency bins.
3. **Smooth Energy Tracking**: The app applies an exponential decay smoothing function:
   `energy[i] = alpha * prev[i] + (1 - alpha) * raw[i]`
4. **Visualizer Rendering**: The frequency values are rendered via reactive Tailwind/SVG bars, animating fluidly at 60 FPS in response to vocal inflection.

---

## Voice Calling Controls

- **Microphone Toggle**: Requests browser `getUserMedia` permissions with automatic echo cancellation and noise suppression.
- **Mode Selector**: Instantly switch between:
  - **User ↔ Agent**: Speak directly with the merchant AI.
  - **Agent ↔ Agent**: Simulate autonomous bot-to-bot acoustic rate discovery.
  - **User ↔ User**: Multi-party conversation with live AI agreement drafting.
- **Simulate Conversation**: For testing environments without a microphone or in headless CI, click **"Simulate Conversation"** to play a synthetic multi-turn audio exchange through the pipeline.

---

## Transcript Logging & AI Scribe

Every utterance during a session is assigned:
- A unique turn ID.
- Speaker role (`Buyer`, `Seller`, `AI Agent`).
- Call duration timestamp (`MM:SS`).
- Semantic tag (`Inquiry`, `Offer`, `Counter-Offer`, `Acceptance`).

### Export Formats
At the bottom of the transcript pane, the **"Download Transcript (.json)"** button generates a complete JSON bundle with full cryptographic metadata, ready for client archiving or legal compliance.
