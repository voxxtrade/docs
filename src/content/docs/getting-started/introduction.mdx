## What is VoxTrade?

At its simplest, **VoxTrade is an open-source protocol that allows people and autonomous AI agents to negotiate, finalize, and pay for services using natural spoken voice on the Stellar network.**

Think about how business was conducted for centuries: two people talking across a table, agreeing on a price, shaking hands, and settling the transaction. Today, online commerce is dominated by silent shopping carts, rigid credit card forms, and slow settlement layers that take 3% cuts.

VoxTrade brings the nuance of human conversation back to modern commerce — but supercharges it with:
1. **Autonomous AI Agents** that can negotiate terms, inventory, and pricing in real time.
2. **Cryptographic Escrows** that guarantee you only pay for what was actually delivered.
3. **Sub-Cent Settlement on Stellar** that makes per-second or per-milestone payments feasible without drowning in network fees.

## How VoxTrade Fixes It

VoxTrade solves these problems with a unified tripartite architecture:

1. **Acoustic Voice Negotiation**:
   Users and AI agents speak naturally over high-clarity WebRTC audio streams. Real-time audio visualizers display speech energy, while an intelligent agent understands nuance, asks clarifying questions, and proposes commercial terms.

2. **Autonomous AI Agreement Synthesis**:
   When negotiation concludes, an AI synthesis engine automatically digests the entire audio conversation, extracts agreed-upon deliverables, price, and deadlines, and outputs both:
   - A human-readable legal contract draft.
   - A cryptographic digest used to initialize an on-chain escrow on Stellar.

3. **HTTP 402 & HTLC Soroban Escrows**:
   Payment is locked in a Soroban smart contract (`X402Escrow`). As speech or services are streamed, micro-payments are unlocked using cryptographic SHA-256 preimages. If either party drops out, the funds automatically refund after a set number of Stellar ledgers.

4. **Guarded Agent Treasuries**:
   Instead of giving AI agents unrestricted private keys, merchants fund an `AgentTreasury` contract on Soroban. The agent is strictly constrained by rolling 24-hour spending caps (`current_ledger / 17280`). The human merchant retains ultimate authority to revoke, pause, or reclaim funds at any second.
